# Banshee
**Data:** `banshee.ts` · RARE · MYTHOLOGY/UNDEAD/MAGE/CURSE · 100 HP

## Identity / win condition
The keening woman. **Keening**: whenever anyone dies, her team gains 1 Chaos. **Wail** hits everyone for 10 and frightens them; **Death's Portent** curses and weakens; **The Keen** silences an enemy for a turn (cooldown 4); Mourning Veil gives an ally Damage Reduction 20.

## Counterplay
100 HP and mostly small effects. Fear is a marker status, so the Wail's real cost is the damage. A dispel clears the curse. Her energy only grows when something dies.

## Readability
Each ability is one clear effect.

## Deviations
Fear and Curse have no engine hook of their own (OQ-72). Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region4.scenario.test.ts`.
