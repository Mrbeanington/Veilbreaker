# The Wild Huntsman
**Data:** `the-wild-huntsman.ts` · SECRET · MYTHOLOGY/ATTACKER/CONTROLLER/SECRET · 140 HP

## Identity / win condition
One quarry at a time. **Sound the Horn** Marks an enemy for 3 turns and weakens it; **Hound's Bite** is 20, or **50 against the Marked**; **Ride Down** is 30 and stuns a Marked enemy; Storm Ride makes him untargetable for a turn.

## Counterplay
Everything strong needs the Mark, which takes an action first and lasts 3 turns; a dispel removes it. Untargetable and stun stop the hunt. DISCOVERABLE secret with a silhouette prompt.

## Readability
The Mark is a printed status and every payoff names it.

## Deviations
`status.mark` was a marker with no hook: here it is the condition every payoff reads. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region4.scenario.test.ts`.
