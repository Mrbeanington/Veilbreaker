# Hydra
**Data:** `hydra.ts` · CORE · BEAST/MYTHOLOGY/BRUISER · 150 HP

## Identity / win condition
Heads (1–5, starts 3) drive Serpent Bite (12 / 25 / 40 damage at 1 / 3 / 5 heads). Every wound costs a head; Regrow buys back two plus 20 HP. Wins by staying at 5 heads.

## Counterplay
Frequent small hits and DoT keep it at 1 head; **Patient Zero**'s Infection ticks count as wounds. Burn cauterizes: no Regrow while burning (no Burn user implemented yet — a real gap noted in the coverage matrix). **Plague Doctor**'s Anti-Heal blanks Regrow's heal half.

## Readability
Heads are a visible pip resource. Bite tiers are a plain ladder.

## Deviations
Head loss keys on any damage (the DoT-as-self-damage quirk counts); spec/03 says only "heads".
