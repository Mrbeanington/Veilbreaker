# The Ghoul
**Data:** `the-ghoul.ts` · RARE · FOLKLORE/UNDEAD/ANTI-HEALER · 120 HP

## Identity / win condition
The graveyard-eater. **Feast on the Fallen**: whenever anyone falls (either team) it heals 10. Gnaw is 20 and Poison; **Carrion Stench** stops every enemy being healed for a turn; Grave Whisper silences; Shroud of Dirt shields for 30.

## Counterplay
Its heal needs a death, and the Stench is one turn on a 3-turn cooldown. Distinct from **Draugr**, who only feeds on enemy deaths and does not stop healing. A dispel cannot remove the passive.

## Readability
The passive is one line.

## Deviations
Anti-heal for one turn instead of healing reduction, so it does not overlap Draugr's Grave Chill. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region6.scenario.test.ts`.
