# Cerberus
**Data:** `cerberus.ts` · CORE · BEAST/MYTHOLOGY/DEFENDER · 150 HP

## Identity / win condition
**Three Bites** is three separate 10-damage hits, so flat Damage Reduction eats every one of them (armour of 10 or more blanks the ability entirely). Guard the Gate taunts and hardens him; Snarl returns 10 damage to the next attacker; Howl of Hades frightens and weakens. **Gate Guard**: when an ally dies he gains Damage Reduction 10 for 2 turns.

## Counterplay
Three Bites is the whole of his damage, and it is the one ability armour beats: **Tortuga Rex** and **The Bronze Giant** shrug it off. Taunt lasts a turn, and Cost 1 Spirit with a 3-turn cooldown. He is a defender with almost no reach.

## Readability
"Three hits" is written on the ability.

## Deviations
Fear is a marker status (no engine hook); Howl's effect is the Weakness it also applies. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region1.scenario.test.ts`.
