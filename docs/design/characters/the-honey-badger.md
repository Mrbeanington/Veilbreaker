# The Honey Badger
**Data:** `the-honey-badger.ts` · CORE · FOLKLORE/BEAST/BRUISER · 120 HP

## Identity / win condition
It simply does not care. **Doesn't Care**: below half health every wound gives it Damage Reduction 10 for a turn. Rip and Tear is 20 and Bleed; Frenzied Bite is 30; **Shrug It Off** lifts every harmful effect from it and heals 10; Dig In and Glare gives Damage Reduction 20.

## Counterplay
The passive only helps once it is already below half health, and Shrug It Off is a 3-turn cooldown. Affliction damage ignores Damage Reduction. Burst kills it at 120 HP.

## Readability
The passive is one line.

## Deviations
None. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region8.scenario.test.ts`.
