# The Scarecrow
**Data:** `the-scarecrow.ts` · CORE · FOLKLORE/TANK/BRUISER · 130 HP

## Identity / win condition
A field guardian. **Straw Stuffing**: at the end of each of its turns it heals 10. Pitchfork is 30; Crow Flock is 10 to every enemy; Field of Fear frightens and weakens; Stand Stiff takes 30 less damage for a turn.

## Counterplay
The regeneration is small and is stopped by Anti-Heal (**Plague Doctor**). Fear is a marker status, so Field of Fear's real effect is the weakness. Burst damage in one turn beats the trickle.

## Readability
One passive line and four numbers.

## Deviations
Fear has no engine rule (OQ-86); the straw does not burn any easier than other targets (no damage types). Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region7.scenario.test.ts`.
