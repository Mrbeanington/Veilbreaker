# The Wandering Genie
**Data:** `the-wandering-genie.ts` · RARE · FOLKLORE/MAGE/SUPPORT · 110 HP

## Identity / win condition
Three wishes and no more. He starts with 3 **Wishes**. Wish for Wealth gives the team 1 Might, Focus and Spirit; Wish for Life heals an ally 40; Wish for Ruin is 50 damage. Each spends a Wish; with none left the first two do nothing and Ruin is only 10. Lamp Light is always 20.

## Counterplay
A wish is spent on use, so he is a front-loaded kit: kill him early or wait him out. The pips are visible. Wishes cannot be recovered.

## Readability
The Wish counter is a pip display and every ability says what happens without one.

## Deviations
First kit whose abilities fizzle when a resource is empty. Not to be confused with **Desert Djinn**, who has no limit and a random outcome. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region6.scenario.test.ts`.
