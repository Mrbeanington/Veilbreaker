# The Roc
**Data:** `the-roc.ts` · RARE · FOLKLORE/BEAST/BRUISER · 160 HP

## Identity / win condition
The giant bird. Talon Strike is 30; **Wingbeat** is 10 to every enemy; **Carry Off** is 30 damage and heals the Roc 20 (life transfer); Roost heals 20.

## Counterplay
Life transfer is not blocked by Damage Reduction or shields (it heals off the hit), but Anti-Heal stops the healing. Big HP but every strong action is a 3-turn cooldown.

## Readability
Numbers are printed.

## Deviations
None. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region6.scenario.test.ts`.
