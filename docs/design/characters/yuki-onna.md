# Yuki-Onna
**Data:** `yuki-onna.ts` · RARE · FOLKLORE/MAGE/CONTROLLER · 100 HP

## Identity / win condition
Ice as tempo. **Frost Breath** hits every enemy for 10 and Chills them (cooldowns recover slower for 2 turns). **Kiss of Winter** is 20 and a Chill on a clean enemy but **30 and a stun on a chilled one**. Vanish into Snow hides and heals her; Winter Lullaby weakens.

## Counterplay
The freeze needs two abilities in a row and Spirit, and she has 100 HP. Anything that clears statuses (Dispel) ruins the set-up; a stun on her stops it. The Chill is the ordinary Cooldown Increase status.

## Readability
"Chilled" is a printed status; the payoff names it.

## Deviations
Ice has no elemental typing in the engine; the Chill is a status. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region2.scenario.test.ts`.
