# The Midnight Tsar
**Data:** `the-midnight-tsar.ts` · SECRET · FOLKLORE/CONTROLLER/UNDEAD/SECRET · 150 HP

## Identity / win condition
The roster's first **relic** user. **Collected Regalia**: every blow he lands recovers a Relic (crown, scepter, orb), up to three. **Crown's Command** spends one to stun; **Scepter Strike** spends two for 60 damage (20 without); **Orb of Midnight** spends all three for 30 damage and a curse on every enemy.

## Counterplay
He must hit first: a stunned or untargetable Tsar collects nothing. The Relics are a visible pip resource, and every payoff is on a cooldown. DISCOVERABLE secret with a silhouette prompt.

## Readability
Relics are pips; each decree prints its cost.

## Deviations
Relics are a resource (no destructible relic objects); see OQ-32. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region3.scenario.test.ts`.
