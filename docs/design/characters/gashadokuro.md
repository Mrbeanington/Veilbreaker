# Gashadokuro
**Data:** `gashadokuro.ts` · RARE · FOLKLORE/UNDEAD/TANK/BRUISER · 160 HP

## Identity / win condition
A giant made of hunger. **Starving Bones**: he gains a Hunger every turn and, from three, is weakened by 10 until he feeds. Bone Fist is 30; **Grasp of the Starved** stuns (cooldown 5); **Devour** is 20 damage and heals 20, or with three Hunger 60 damage and heals 30 and spends the Hunger; Rattling Dread frightens and weakens.

## Counterplay
Feeding is cheap, but a Hunger of three means Devour lands at 50, not 60, because of his own weakness. Kill him before he feeds, or refuse the stun. **The Plague Doctor**'s Anti-Heal blanks Devour's healing.

## Readability
One pip, one payoff; the weakness is stated in the passive.
