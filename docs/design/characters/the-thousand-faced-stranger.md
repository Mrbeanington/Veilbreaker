# The Thousand-Faced Stranger
**Data:** `the-thousand-faced-stranger.ts` · SECRET · FOLKLORE/CONTROLLER/ATTACKER/SECRET · 120 HP

## Identity / win condition
A wanderer with a face for every road. **Face of Wrath** is 30, or 50 right after the Face of Fear; **Face of Mercy** heals an ally 30 right after Wrath, otherwise 10; **Face of Fear** is 10 damage, or a stun right after Mercy; Blank Face makes him untargetable for a turn. The faces chain: wrath, mercy, fear, wrath.

## Counterplay
The loop takes three turns to come round, and each link is visible. Interrupt it (stun, silence) and every ability is weak. DISCOVERABLE secret with a silhouette prompt.

## Readability
Every ability names the face that powers it.

## Deviations
A rotation of three abilities, not a transformation: it needs no engine support. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region6.scenario.test.ts`.
