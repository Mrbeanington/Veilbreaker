# Sand Assassin
**Data:** `sand-assassin.ts` · RARE · MYTHOLOGY/ASSASSIN/ATTACKER · 100 HP

## Identity / win condition
A blade that vanishes. Dust Blade is 20; **Shifting Dune** makes him untargetable for a turn; **Ambush** is 30, or **70 right after Shifting Dune**; Poison Kris is 10 and Poison.

## Counterplay
The Ambush is a two-turn plan the enemy can read: the vanish is a visible turn. Area attacks and damage over time hit him anyway, and 100 HP means one burst kills him.

## Readability
The two-turn rhythm is printed on Ambush.

## Deviations
None. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region5.scenario.test.ts`.
