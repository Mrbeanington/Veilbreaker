# Fenris
**Data:** `fenris.ts` · RARE · MYTHOLOGY/BEAST/ATTACKER · 160 HP

## Identity / win condition
The chained wolf: the opposite of a slow build. He starts with three **Chains**; **Links Break** removes one every time he is wounded. Snap is 20; Howl of the Bound weakens everyone; **Strain at the Chain** costs 10 health for a link; **Ragnarök Bite** is 30 while bound and **70 once no links are left**.

## Counterplay
He does not have to be hit to be free (Strain) but that costs health; a plan that never touches him leaves the bite at 30. The chain count is a visible pip.

## Readability
"Broken chain: 70" is printed.

## Deviations
None. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region4.scenario.test.ts`.
