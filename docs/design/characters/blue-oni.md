# Blue Oni
**Data:** `blue-oni.ts` · CORE · FOLKLORE/DEFENDER/CONTROLLER · 150 HP

## Identity / win condition
The quiet half. Cold Iron Bar is 20 damage; **Cool Head** shields an ally for 20 (40 with Red Oni on the team); Draw the Fire taunts and shields him for 20; Icy Grip is 10 damage and slows cooldowns. **Brothers' Bond**: with Red Oni beside him he mends 10 at every turn start.

## Counterplay
Alone he is only a modest defender. A shield is absorbed damage, so a single big hit or a Dispel strips it. His taunt lasts one turn and is on a 3-turn cooldown.

## Readability
Numbers on every ability; the bonus is named in the text.

## Deviations
None. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region2.scenario.test.ts`.
