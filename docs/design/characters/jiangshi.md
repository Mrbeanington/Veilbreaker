# Jiangshi
**Data:** `jiangshi.ts` · CORE · FOLKLORE/UNDEAD/BRUISER · 140 HP

## Identity / win condition
The hopping corpse. Grave Claw is 20 and Bleed; Hopping Strike is 30; **Qi Drain** steals 1 energy from the enemy team for itself; Rigor gives Damage Reduction 20. It wins by starving the enemy of energy while hitting hard.

## Counterplay
Qi Drain costs Chaos and a 2-turn cooldown and takes a single energy, so it never fully starves a team. Stun and silence stop the plan. It is a plain bruiser otherwise.

## Readability
Four abilities, four numbers.

## Deviations
The paper-charm weakness of the legend is not modelled; it is a bruiser with an energy thief. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region6.scenario.test.ts`.
