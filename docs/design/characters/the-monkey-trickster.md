# The Monkey Trickster
**Data:** `the-monkey-trickster.ts` · RARE · FOLKLORE/ATTACKER/ASSASSIN · 120 HP

## Identity / win condition
The stone monkey with the staff and the cloud. Staff Strike is 30; **Pluck Hairs** summons a clone with 20 health for 3 turns; Cloud Somersault makes him untargetable for a turn; **Steal a Peach** is 10 damage and heals him 20.

## Counterplay
The clone is small and area damage clears it. Somersault is a 3-turn cooldown. His damage is modest; a kit that ignores his tricks out-trades him.

## Readability
One effect per ability.

## Deviations
The clone reuses the pet shape (a health pool, a duration, no team slot); it has no actions of its own (OQ-75). Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region6.scenario.test.ts`.
