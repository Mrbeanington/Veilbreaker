# Emperor Zero (Legend)
**Data:** `emperor-zero.ts` · LEGENDARY · CONTROLLER · 130 HP

## Identity / win condition
Cooldown control and near-removal. **Revoke** locks the enemy's *most recently used* ability for six turns (Ability Lock with the generic `@lastUsed` token, ADR-015), on a 5-turn cooldown. **Stasis Field** freezes every enemy cooldown for two turns. Cold Logic is 25 piercing; Iron Decree is Damage Reduction 20. **Failsafe Protocol** (passive): below 40% HP every wound makes Revoke ready again.

## Balance levers
Revoke is expensive (2 Focus + 1 Chaos), long-cooldown, finite (six turns, not permanent) and only ever hits what the target just used. Only 130 HP.

## Counterplay — concrete counters from the roster
- **Father Bell**: Hush silences him, so Revoke cannot be cast (tested).
- **Baiting**: end the turn with a throwaway ability and Revoke locks the decoy (tested). Any character with a cheap filler can do this.
- **Mister Whiskers**: energy theft starves the Focus and Chaos Revoke needs.

## Readability
"Whatever you used last is banned." Fully visible on the target's status bar with its remaining turns.

## Deviations
"Nearly remove" is a six-turn lock; a true permanent removal would be uncounterable. Ability Lock holds a single ability at a time, so a second Revoke replaces the first.
