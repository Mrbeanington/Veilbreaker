# Father Frost
**Data:** `father-frost.ts` · RARE · FOLKLORE/TANK/CONTROLLER · 170 HP

## Identity / win condition
The winter host. Staff Rap is 30; **Frozen Gift** shields an ally for 30 and strengthens healing on them; **Bitter Cold** makes every enemy's abilities cost 1 more for 2 turns; **Winter's Judgement** is 20 and a weakness, then 30 and a stun on the weakened.

## Counterplay
Mostly slow: his best play needs two Judgements. Energy denial is a mirror of his own tax. A shield is absorbed damage that one big hit strips.

## Readability
Two statuses are named, and the payoff names the first.

## Deviations
Ice is flavour (Chill and cost increase), not an elemental type. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region3.scenario.test.ts`.
