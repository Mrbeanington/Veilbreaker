# The White Fox
**Data:** `the-white-fox.ts` · RARE · FOLKLORE/MAGE/SUPPORT · 100 HP

## Identity / win condition
Kindness with teeth. Fox Bite is 20; **Kitsune Grace** heals an ally 20 and lifts every harmful effect; **Bewitch** stuns for a turn; Mirror Trick throws damage taken back for a turn.

## Counterplay
Bewitch costs Focus and Spirit on a 4-turn cooldown; affliction and damage-over-time ignore the mirror. 100 HP and small numbers.

## Readability
One effect per ability.

## Deviations
None. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region6.scenario.test.ts`.
