# Zmey Gorynych
**Data:** `zmey-gorynych.ts` · RARE · FOLKLORE/BEAST/ATTACKER/BRUISER · 130 HP

## Identity / win condition
Three heads, three abilities: **Left Head** (10 and Burn), **Middle Head** (30), **Right Head** (10 and poison). **Roar of Three** is 30 damage, or **70 and a stun** if his last three abilities were Left, Middle, Right in that order (cooldown 5).

## Counterplay
The 70 takes three turns in a fixed order and one interruption resets it. Each head has a different cost family (Focus, Might, Chaos), so any energy denial cuts the chain.

## Readability
The sequence is printed on the Roar.

## Deviations
Like the Siren's song, the sequence reads the character's own last abilities. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region3.scenario.test.ts`.
