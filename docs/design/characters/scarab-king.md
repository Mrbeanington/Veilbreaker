# The Scarab King
**Data:** `scarab-king.ts` · RARE · MYTHOLOGY/BEAST/SUMMONER · 120 HP

## Identity / win condition
A swarm engine. **Skittering Horde**: every ability he uses feeds the Swarm by 1 (max 4). **Scarab Tide** is 30, or **60 with 3 Swarm**, spending them. Call the Swarm summons a scarab swarm that soaks 30 damage for 3 turns; Carapace gives Damage Reduction 20.

## Counterplay
He must act three times before the big hit, and the Swarm is a visible pip. A stun or silence resets the plan; burst damage kills him at 120 HP before the Tide lands. Area damage clears his swarm.

## Readability
The resource is a pip counter and Scarab Tide prints both numbers.

## Deviations
The swarm summon reuses the Paper Monk's pet shape (a health pool and a duration, no team slot). Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region5.scenario.test.ts`.
