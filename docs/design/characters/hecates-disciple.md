# Hecate's Disciple
**Data:** `hecates-disciple.ts` · RARE · MYTHOLOGY/MAGE/CURSE/RANDOM · 110 HP

## Identity / win condition
Crossroads magic. Moonless Torch is 10 damage and Burn. **Three Roads** lays one of four hexes at random (weights 2/2/2/1): weakened, slowed, more expensive, or silenced for a turn. Witch's Ward gives an ally Damage Reduction 10 for 3 turns. **Dark Supper** deals 20 and curses, or 50 to an already-cursed enemy.

## Counterplay
The random hex is mostly small (three of four outcomes are mild); the silence is the rare one. Dark Supper needs a curse first (**Nemesis**'s Curse of Hubris also sets it up). Frail at 110 HP.

## Readability
"Curse it, then feed on the curse".

## Deviations
Curse is a marker status: Dark Supper is the only thing that reads it. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region1.scenario.test.ts`.
