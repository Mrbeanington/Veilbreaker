# The Moon Rabbit
**Data:** `the-moon-rabbit.ts` · RARE · FOLKLORE/HEALER/SUPPORT · 100 HP

## Identity / win condition
The patient healer. **Elixir of Life** heals an ally for 30 (Spirit 1); **Moon Glow** amplifies every heal on the whole team for 3 turns; Lucky Foot shields; Mortar Pound is 20.

## Counterplay
All her healing costs Spirit, and she has 100 HP; a team that drains or locks Spirit starves her. Anti-heal and healing reduction (**Plague Doctor**) blank her whole kit.

## Readability
Heals are printed numbers.

## Deviations
None. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region6.scenario.test.ts`.
