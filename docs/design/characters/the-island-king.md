# The Island King (BACKLOG — design note only, not built)
**Status:** not in the numbered 120; may enter an expansion or replace a launch character during balancing (spec/03). No data, art or tests yet.

## Concept
A feral, sunburnt young ruler of a stranded shore: barefoot, painted with clay and ash, crowned with a driftwood-and-bone circlet, carrying a sharpened staff. He rules by fear and by keeping a following together. Original creation, inspired only by the general idea of survival stories and primitive social hierarchy. **Do not** use any existing novel's names, characters, dialogue, plot, costumes or iconography (no conch, glasses, beast, "pig" imagery, or anything that reads as an adaptation).

## Proposed identity (for a future session)
- **Followers** (resource, 0-4): rise when allies act together (a combo sequence) or when an enemy is Feared; each Follower is a small, visible, temporary "fourth fighter"-style presence (a summon that occupies no slot) or a numeric pip. Decide which when the summon rules for non-slot pets are settled (OQ-32 territory).
- **Fear** (status on enemies): a Fearing enemy deals less damage; the King's payoff spends Followers for a team-wide push.
- Hierarchy twist: if he is defeated, his Followers scatter (the pips drop to zero and the summons expire), so killing the King is the counter.

## Counterplay to plan for
Target the King first (his HP should be low), dispel or silence Fear, and any AoE that kills small summons. A cheater-free character with a clean "kill the head" counter.

## Cautions
Handle tone with care: a story about children turned cruel must not become gore or cruelty for its own sake; keep it stylised. Cultural and literary-reference review is required before any art (OQ-12).
