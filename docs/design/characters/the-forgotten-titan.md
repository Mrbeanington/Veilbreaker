# The Forgotten Titan
**Data:** `the-forgotten-titan.ts` · SECRET · MYTHOLOGY/TANK/EVOLUTION/SECRET · 150 HP

## Identity / win condition
A sleeper: **Stirring** (0-5) rises by one every turn (**Heavy Sleeper**) and at five the Titan **wakes** (a transformation): the kit changes from Sleeper's Roll / Dreaming Weight / Restless Turn to Titan's Wrath (60), Unbound Roar (30 to every enemy) and the two sleeper moves. While it is asleep (under four Stirring) it takes 10 less damage.

## Counterplay
It is a clock everyone can see. The Titan can speed it (Restless Turn +2) or slow it (Dreaming Weight -1 and Damage Reduction 20). Killing it before turn 5 or denying Might and Spirit stops the awake kit. It is the region's strongest simulated character (see the region-1 report): flagged, not fixed, in this phase.

## Readability
A visible number counting up to waking.
