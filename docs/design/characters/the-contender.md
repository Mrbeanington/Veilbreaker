# The Contender
**Data:** `the-contender.ts` · CORE · ATHLETE/BRUISER/WARRIOR · 140 HP

## Identity / win condition
A boxer who is most dangerous when he is losing. Jab is 20; **Haymaker** is 30, or **80 when he is below 40% health**; Rope-a-Dope strikes back for 20 whenever hit for 2 turns; Cut Man heals 20 and lifts every harmful effect.

## Counterplay
The 80 needs him to be hurt already: a team that never takes him below 40%, or that finishes him from 41% in one burst, never sees it. Cut Man is a 4-turn cooldown. Affliction and damage over time get round Rope-a-Dope.

## Readability
The 40% threshold is printed on the ability.

## Deviations
The comeback lever is on the ability, not the passive, so it is not hidden. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region9.scenario.test.ts`.
