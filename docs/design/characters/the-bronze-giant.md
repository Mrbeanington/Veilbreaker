# The Bronze Giant
**Data:** `the-bronze-giant.ts` · RARE · MYTHOLOGY/TANK/ATTACKER · 160 HP

## Identity / win condition
A slow, armoured island guardian: Hurl Boulder 40, Bronze Plating (Damage Reduction 20 for 2 turns), Circle the Island (20 to every enemy) and Heavy Tread (40 and weakens). **Ichor Vein** is his flaw: while he burns, his bronze softens and he takes 20 more damage that turn.

## Counterplay
The flaw is concrete and on the roster: **The Nine-Tailed Trickster**'s Fox Fire, **Cyclops Brontes**'s Molten Slag and **Hecate's Disciple**'s Moonless Torch all apply Burn. Heavy costs (Might 2-3) and cooldowns; Bronze Plating is his only defence. The passive is DISCOVERABLE.

## Readability
One vulnerability, one number.

## Deviations
Fire has no elemental damage type in the engine; "burns" means the Burn status. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region1.scenario.test.ts`.
