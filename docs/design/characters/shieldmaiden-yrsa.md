# Shieldmaiden Yrsa
**Data:** `shieldmaiden-yrsa.ts` · CORE · MYTHOLOGY/WARRIOR/DEFENDER · 140 HP

## Identity / win condition
The line-holder. Spear Thrust is 20; **Shield Wall** draws single-target attacks and gives Damage Reduction 20 for 2 turns; Cover an Ally shields a friend for 30; **Shield Bash** is 20, or **30 and a stun right after Shield Wall**.

## Counterplay
The stun needs Shield Wall first (a visible, taunting turn). Area attacks ignore the taunt. Multi-hit attackers (**Cerberus**, **Puca**) shred her flat armour.

## Readability
The two-turn rhythm is printed on Shield Bash.

## Deviations
None. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region4.scenario.test.ts`.
