# The Thing Beneath the Bed
**Data:** `the-thing-beneath-the-bed.ts` · RARE · FOLKLORE/ASSASSIN/CURSE · 110 HP

## Identity / win condition
The fear you knew was there. **Grasp from Below** is 30, or **60 against an enemy at full health**; Night Terrors is 10 to every enemy and Fear; Cold Hand stuns; Under the Bed makes it untargetable for a turn.

## Counterplay
The 60 only lands on the first hit against a target, so any prior damage or healing that leaves the enemy below full denies it. At 110 HP one burst kills it.

## Readability
"At full health" is printed on the ability.

## Deviations
Fear is a marker status (OQ-86). Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region7.scenario.test.ts`.
