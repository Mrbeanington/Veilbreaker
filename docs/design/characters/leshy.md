# Leshy
**Data:** `leshy.ts` · CORE · FOLKLORE/CONTROLLER/BEAST · 150 HP

## Identity / win condition
**Growth** (0-3) rises by one every turn (**The Wood Grows**). **Root Snare** stuns for a turn with a full thicket (spending the Growth) and only weakens otherwise. Thorn Volley is 10 damage and poison; Bark Skin gives Damage Reduction 20 for 2 turns; Wild Growth heals 20 and adds a Growth.

## Counterplay
Growth is a visible pip, so the stun is telegraphed. Wild Growth speeds it but costs Spirit and a turn. **Mister Whiskers**'s energy theft and any silence stop the Snare.

## Readability
"Three pips, then it stuns" is the whole rule.

## Deviations
None. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region3.scenario.test.ts`.
