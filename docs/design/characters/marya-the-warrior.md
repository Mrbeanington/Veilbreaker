# Marya the Warrior
**Data:** `marya-the-warrior.ts` · RARE · FOLKLORE/WARRIOR/ATTACKER · 130 HP

## Identity / win condition
A plain hard fighter. Sabre Cut is 20; **Rally** heals 10 and speeds her cooldowns for 2 turns; **Horse Charge** is 30, or **50 right after Rally**; Hold the Line draws single-target attacks for a turn and returns 20 damage to whoever hits her.

## Counterplay
The combo is two turns and Rally is visible. The counter only punishes attackers who choose her; a stun or silence between Rally and Charge breaks it.

## Readability
Numbers and one combo, printed.

## Deviations
None. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region3.scenario.test.ts`.
