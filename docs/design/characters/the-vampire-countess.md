# The Vampire Countess
**Data:** `the-vampire-countess.ts` · RARE · FOLKLORE/UNDEAD/ATTACKER · 120 HP

## Identity / win condition
An aristocratic predator. **Fangs** is 20 and heals her 10 (life transfer); Mist Form makes her untargetable for a turn; **Crimson Kiss** weakens and adds Damage Amplification 10; Bat Swarm is 10 to every enemy.

## Counterplay
Anti-Heal (**Plague Doctor**) turns off her sustain; area damage ignores the mist. 120 HP and small numbers.

## Readability
One effect per ability.

## Deviations
Sunlight and garlic weaknesses are not modelled. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region7.scenario.test.ts`.
