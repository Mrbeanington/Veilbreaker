# Ashmouth
**Data:** `ashmouth.ts` · RARE · FOLKLORE/BEAST/CONTROLLER · 150 HP

## Identity / win condition
A monster with a furnace for a throat. **Choking Ash** makes every ability cost every enemy more for 2 turns; Maw Snap is 30; **Swallow** is 40 damage and heals it 20; Ash Shroud takes 20 less damage.

## Counterplay
Choking Ash costs Spirit and has a 3-turn cooldown; a dispel removes the cost rise. Swallow is Might, Might and Chaos on a 3-turn cooldown, and Anti-Heal stops the heal.

## Readability
Every number is printed.

## Deviations
The cost rise is the only 'tax' effect in the roster that hits a whole team. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region7.scenario.test.ts`.
