# The Mirror Samurai
**Data:** `the-mirror-samurai.ts` · RARE · FOLKLORE/WARRIOR/DEFENDER · 130 HP

## Identity / win condition
Answers a blow with the blow. **Ready Stance** counters for 20; **Polished Guard** reflects damage for a turn; **Perfect Reflection** is 20, or **50 if Polished Guard was his last ability**; Study the Foe shortens his cooldowns.

## Counterplay
Reflect only redirects Normal damage, so Affliction and Piercing (and status effects) go straight through. Polished Guard is one turn on a 3-turn cooldown: a patient enemy waits it out. Overlaps in theme with **Nemesis** and **Zeiron**'s counter, but not in kit (closest kit overlap is reported by the coverage test).

## Readability
Two defensive shapes and one clear payoff.

## Deviations
None. Simulated as a mild outlier (see the region 2 notes). Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region2.scenario.test.ts`.
