# Sir Hopsalot
**Data:** `sir-hopsalot.ts` · CORE · FOLKLORE/BEAST/WARRIOR · 120 HP

## Identity / win condition
A rabbit knight of absolute sincerity. Triple Hop is three hits of 10; **Lance Charge** is 30 and stuns for a turn (Might 2, Focus 1, cooldown 4); Carrot of Valor shields an ally for 30; Knight's Guard makes him strike back for 20 when hit for a turn.

## Counterplay
The stun is a 4-turn cooldown and the numbers are small. Multi-hit attacks are answered by the shield rather than the counter, and 120 HP means one burst kills him.

## Readability
Three hits and three numbers.

## Deviations
Reworked once so it did not overlap **Shieldmaiden Yrsa**: the counter replaces a taunt. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region8.scenario.test.ts`.
