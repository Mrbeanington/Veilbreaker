# Jackal Guardian
**Data:** `jackal-guardian.ts` · CORE · MYTHOLOGY/BEAST/DEFENDER · 140 HP

## Identity / win condition
The tomb's sentinel. Khopesh Slash is 20; **Tomb Ward** returns 20 to anyone who hits him for 2 turns; Sentinel's Howl weakens an intruder; Pounce is 30 and Bleed. He wins by being hit and hitting back.

## Counterplay
Tomb Ward is a 2-turn window with a 3-turn cooldown; area or damage-over-time attacks are not answered in the same way. Stun, silence and simply waiting out the ward beat him. **Nemesis**-style retaliators do the same job better.

## Readability
One ward, one howl, two hits.

## Deviations
None. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region5.scenario.test.ts`.
