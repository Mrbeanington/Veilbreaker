# The Living Sarcophagus
**Data:** `the-living-sarcophagus.ts` · RARE · MYTHOLOGY/TANK/CONTROLLER · 180 HP

## Identity / win condition
A stone coffin that walks. Lid Slam is 30; **Entomb** seals an enemy for a turn (stunned and untargetable, so allies cannot heal or shield it); **Lid Closed** reflects damage taken for a turn; Grave Dust weakens every enemy.

## Counterplay
Reflect is a single turn on a 4-turn cooldown; **Nemesis**-style effects and area damage aside, a bot simply waits it out. Damage over time and affliction ignore the reflect.

## Readability
Three abilities, each a single clear effect.

## Deviations
None. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region5.scenario.test.ts`.
