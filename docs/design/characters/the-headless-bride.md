# The Headless Bride
**Data:** `the-headless-bride.ts` · RARE · FOLKLORE/UNDEAD/CURSE · 110 HP

## Identity / win condition
A vow that outlived her. **Widow's Grief**: whenever an ally falls she gains a Grief (max 3). **Vengeful Wail** is 30, or **60 with Grief**, which it spends. Veil Lash is 20; Bouquet Toss shields a friend for 30; Jilted Vow Marks an enemy and weakens it.

## Counterplay
She needs an ally to fall first, so her best turns come when her team is already losing. The Grief pips are visible, the wail is a Chaos and Might cost on a 2-turn cooldown, and stun or silence stops it. With no Grief she is a 110-HP support.

## Readability
The pips and both wail numbers are printed.

## Deviations
The Mark (a marker status with no rule of its own) is only a label here; the weakness does the work. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region7.scenario.test.ts`.
