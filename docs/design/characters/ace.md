# Ace
**Data:** `ace.ts` · RARE · ATHLETE/ATTACKER/ASSASSIN · 100 HP

## Identity / win condition
A tennis pro whose serve cannot be blocked. **Serve** is 20 **piercing** damage (ignores shields and Damage Reduction); **Match Point** is 60 piercing (Might 2, Focus 2, a 4-turn cooldown); Volley strikes back for 20 for a turn; Drop Shot is 10 and weakens.

## Counterplay
Piercing ignores armour but not Invulnerable, untargetability or stun; she has 100 HP and a short list of answers to a counter. **Tortuga Rex**'s Damage Reduction does nothing against her, but a stun on her stops everything.

## Readability
"Piercing" is printed on both numbers.

## Deviations
A kit built around piercing damage (the damage type has existed since phase 02). Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region9.scenario.test.ts`.
