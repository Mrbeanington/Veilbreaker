# The Paper Monk
**Data:** `the-paper-monk.ts` · RARE · FOLKLORE/SUPPORT/SUMMONER/HEALER · 110 HP

## Identity / win condition
Healing and paper. **Prayer Slip** heals an ally 20; **Fold a Crane** summons a **Paper Crane Familiar** (30 HP, 3 turns, no team slot: the roster's first pet); Seal Charm silences an enemy; **Ink Blot** is 10 damage and weakens. Ink and paper are the theme (an "ink" mechanic on the coverage list).

## Counterplay
The crane is a small fragile decoy that any AoE removes. Seal Charm is a single 1-turn silence on a 4-turn cooldown. He is a healer without a shield.

## Readability
Everything is a number.

## Deviations
The familiar has no abilities of its own yet (it is a body that takes damage); giving pets actions is a later engine step (OQ-75). Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region2.scenario.test.ts`.
