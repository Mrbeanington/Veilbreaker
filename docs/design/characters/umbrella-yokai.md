# Umbrella Yokai
**Data:** `umbrella-yokai.ts` · RARE · FOLKLORE/CONTROLLER/DEFENDER · 110 HP

## Identity / win condition
A hopping nuisance. Hop Kick is 20; **Tongue Lash** licks 1 energy from an enemy and weakens it; **Open Umbrella** shelters an ally (Damage Reduction 20 for 2 turns); **Rain Dance** hits every enemy for 10 and makes their next turn cost 1 more.

## Counterplay
Nothing he does is decisive; he wins by adding small taxes. Silence stops all of it, and Open Umbrella is flat armour, which multi-hit attackers shred.

## Readability
Each ability says exactly what it takes.

## Deviations
None. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region2.scenario.test.ts`.
