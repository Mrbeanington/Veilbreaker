# Ifrit
**Data:** `ifrit.ts` · RARE · MYTHOLOGY/MAGE/ATTACKER · 100 HP

## Identity / win condition
Fire that feeds on fire. Flame Lash is 20 and Burn 5; Cinder Storm burns every enemy; **Molten Fury** is 20, or **40 against a burning enemy**. Cleanse and healing shrug off the burn.

## Counterplay
The 40 needs an active burn, so a dispel (**The Firebird**), a heal over the burn or Damage Reduction blunts him. Its damage all costs Might, so **Frost Jotunn**'s Might lock stops the whole kit.

## Readability
"Burning" is a printed status on the enemy.

## Deviations
None. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region5.scenario.test.ts`.
