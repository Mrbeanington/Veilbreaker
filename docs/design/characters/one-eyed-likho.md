# One-Eyed Likho
**Data:** `one-eyed-likho.ts` · RARE · FOLKLORE/CURSE/CONTROLLER/RANDOM · 130 HP

## Identity / win condition
Misfortune. **Evil Eye** curses and weakens; Ill Omen raises costs and slows cooldowns; **Wandering Woe** is a random 10, 30 or 50 damage (weights 4, 2, 1); **Shared Misfortune** is 50 damage on a cursed enemy at a cost of 20 of her own health, and does nothing otherwise.

## Counterplay
Every strong play needs the curse and costs her health. The random hit is mostly the small one. A dispel undoes the curse.

## Readability
The condition is printed on the ability.

## Deviations
None. The ability is named LIKHO_EVIL_EYE in code only because `EVIL_EYE` already exists for Baba Yaga. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region3.scenario.test.ts`.
