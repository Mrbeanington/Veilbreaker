# Arachne
**Data:** `arachne.ts` · RARE · MYTHOLOGY/CONTROLLER/ASSASSIN · 110 HP

## Identity / win condition
**Threads** (0-4) come from Spin Web, Snare Line, and from being hit (**Web Sense**). Spin Web slows an enemy's cooldowns; Snare Line raises its energy costs; Venom Fang poisons. **Tapestry** is 30 damage, and with three Threads it also stuns for a turn and spends them.

## Counterplay
She wins by taxing what enemies can do, not by damage, and she is fragile (110 HP). A stun needs three Threads, which is visible. Two of her tools are pure status effects (cooldown increase, cost increase): a dispeller or a fast kill undoes them.

## Readability
Threads are pips; Tapestry says exactly what three Threads does.
