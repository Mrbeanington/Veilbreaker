# The Collector
**Data:** `the-collector.ts` · RARE · FOLKLORE/CONTROLLER/ASSASSIN · 120 HP

## Identity / win condition
A connoisseur of the damaged. Cane Strike is 20; **Appraise** is 20, or **50 against an enemy that has already taken 60 or more damage** this battle; **Pickpocket** lifts 2 Spirit from the enemy team; Glass Case stuns for a turn.

## Counterplay
He wants enemies that others have already hurt, so he needs partners who deal damage first. Pickpocket is Chaos-only with a 3-turn cooldown and takes Spirit only.

## Readability
The threshold is printed on Appraise.

## Deviations
Distinct from **Anubian Judge** (who punishes damage an enemy has dealt): the Collector rewards damage an enemy has taken. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region7.scenario.test.ts`.
