# General Goose
**Data:** `general-goose.ts` · RARE · FOLKLORE/BEAST/SUPPORT · 140 HP

## Identity / win condition
A veteran who is genuinely dangerous. Peck is 20; **Fixed Bayonet** is 40 and Bleed; Commanding Honk weakens every enemy for 2 turns; **Reveille** speeds cooldown recovery for every ally for 2 turns.

## Counterplay
Reveille and the Honk are 3-turn cooldowns and cost Spirit; a dispel removes either. The Goose is a support that hits hard, so a team that kills it early loses the tempo.

## Readability
One clear effect per ability.

## Deviations
First kit to use Cooldown Reduction on the whole team. Tested in `packages/engine/src/scenarios/region8.scenario.test.ts`.
