# Balance report — 2026-09-19 (Phase 07 first pass)

> **Recommendations only.** No balance number was changed. Three 10,000-match runs over the 20 playable prototypes (random 3v3 teams, 40-turn limit, seed 1), one per bot level, all with **0 engine errors**. Raw output: `report-2026-09-19-{beginner,intermediate,expert}.{md,json}`. Reproduce with `pnpm sim --matches 10000 --bots expert --seed 1`.

## Recommendations

1. **Second player has a large advantage (about 64/36).** The player moving first on turn 1 wins only 36–40% of decided games, and the gap widens as the bots get smarter. **Confirmed cause:** OQ-03's rule that the initiative player gets no energy on turn 1, combined with 1 random energy per living character per turn: it costs the first player a whole turn of options, and resolving first within a tier is worth little. A 4,000-match INTERMEDIATE run with `initiativePlayerSkipsTurnOneGeneration: false` moved the first player's win rate from 37.1% to 49.5%, so dropping (or softening) that rule closes the gap. Energy lockouts barely changed (4,964 to 4,840), so they are a separate problem. (OQ-44)
2. **Energy starvation is the most common degenerate pattern.** At INTERMEDIATE, 675 of 1,000 sampled matches contain a team with no legal action for three turns in a row (12,394 detector hits over 10,000 matches; 78 of the sampled matches are stuck from turn 1 to 3). With 1 random energy per living character and a 10-per-type cap, expensive kits (Zeiron's 8-cost Wrath, Fracture, Da Capo) are hard to pay for, and a team down to one survivor generates almost nothing. Consider a higher base rate, a catch-up rule, or lower costs on cheap abilities. Do not change all of them: situational cost is part of the identity of the Legends. (OQ-45)
3. **Patient Zero and Hydra look dominant.** Patient Zero beats 10 of 19 opponents 75%+ of the time at EXPERT (9 at INTERMEDIATE, 16 at BEGINNER); Hydra does so against 4, 8 and 14 opponents. Both are self-scaling (evolution, extra heads). Look at the speed of Patient Zero's auto-evolution and Hydra's head growth against the 40-turn game; do not nerf until the cause is confirmed. (OQ-46)
4. **Maestro Nocturne is the weakest pick (about 34%).** His kit is support with 100 HP and low damage; the bots probably under-use Allegro and Da Capo because they score mostly by immediate effect, so this number may understate him. Consider raising Staccato damage or making Adagio also deal damage to all enemies. (OQ-46)
5. **Legends are not runaway.** Teams with a Legend win 48.5–50.2% across the three levels. Individually at EXPERT: The Nameless One 55.1%, Black Knight 54.1%, Shiro 49.8%, Behemoth 45.4%, Zeiron 41.5%, Emperor Zero 41.4%. Zeiron and Emperor Zero look under-powered for a Legend given their costs, in line with recommendation 2.
6. **Turn-limit rate is 11–15%** (1,140–1,463 of 10,000 matches). Not alarming; the cause was not analysed this pass. Energy starvation (2) is a likely contributor.
7. **The safety rails held.** No state loops, perpetual stuns, resurrection loops, rewind loops, illegal bot actions, engine errors or stalls in 30,000 matches. One "one-turn kill" of a full-health character per run, effectively noise.

## Caveats
- Bots are heuristic; they do not know character-specific combos, so combo-heavy characters (Maestro, Gambler, Referee) are probably under-rated.
- Teams are uniformly random from the pool, not meta-aware. Counter matrix cells have about 450 games each, roughly ±4.5 points of noise.
- Same seed across levels, so team draws are identical between runs; differences are due to bot skill.

## Decision taken in Phase 08: energy rules changed
The project owner delegated OQ-44 and OQ-45. Chosen (in `packages/content/src/config/energy-rules.json`): `initiativePlayerSkipsTurnOneGeneration: false` and `perLivingCharacter: 2` (was `true` and `1`). Chosen by simulating variants first (3,000 matches each, INTERMEDIATE): skip-off alone fixed the first-player gap but not lockouts; two energy per living character cut lockouts to 2.7% of matches and made games shorter and more decisive.

Re-run after the change (`report-2026-09-19-after-energy-{intermediate,expert}.md`, 10,000 and 3,000 matches, 0 engine errors):

| Metric | Before (INTERMEDIATE) | After (INTERMEDIATE) | After (EXPERT) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| First player wins | 36.8% | 50.3% | 50.4% |
| Average match length | 25.2 turns | 16.1 | 14.9 |
| Turn-limit hits | 1,463 / 10,000 | 325 / 10,000 | 84 / 3,000 |
| Energy-lockout hits | 12,394 | 4,018 | 1,198 |
| Teams with a Legend win | 50.2% | 52.5% | 51.4% |

Effects on individual picks: Hydra fell out of the top group (recommendation 3 partly resolved). Zeiron recovered at INTERMEDIATE (46% to 50%) but stays low at EXPERT (41.6%). **New watch items:** Black Knight now wins 66-70% and The Nameless One about 60%, both more than before, and Patient Zero is still 74-77%; Maestro Nocturne, The Referee, Moonshot Maddox and Malachar are still at or below 38%. These are recommendations only (OQ-48).
