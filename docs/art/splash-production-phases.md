# Splash art production phases

119 unique splash illustrations across 11 phases. Legends are produced first and skipped in later origin groups. Checkboxes below track splash completion, not portrait completion.

Each splash: one individual <id>.splash.png, tall 2:3, at least 768 pixels wide; match the approved portrait identity and painting style. Keep the complete character silhouette inside the frame and omit all writing. Preserve source cultural-review flags.

Before generation, reconcile scene descriptions with the global plain-background requirement. Also resolve prompt requests for glyphs, notation or jersey numbers against the no-writing rule. Character-specific anatomy takes priority over generic human head-to-feet wording.
