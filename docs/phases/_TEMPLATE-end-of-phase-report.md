# End-of-phase report (use at the end of every phase)
1. **Summary:** what now works, in 3–6 sentences.
2. **Files created:** full paths.
3. **Files changed:** full paths plus a one-line reason for each.
4. **Tests:** count added, total passing, and the commands run.
5. **Deviations from spec:** what changed and why (also logged in DECISIONS.md).
6. **New open questions:** added to OPEN-QUESTIONS.md.
7. **Custom scripts added:** registered in DECISIONS.md, or "none".
8. **Recommended next step.**
Then update PROGRESS.md and STOP.
