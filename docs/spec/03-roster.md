# 03 — Roster

## Structure
- V1 target: 120 characters. Architecture must support 500+ without restructuring data formats or save files; expansions target roughly 300–400.
- Categories: **CORE, RARE, SECRET, LEGENDARY.** Rarity is not power: Core characters can counter Secret and Legendary characters.
- Secret characters have unusual mechanics and unusual unlock conditions.
- There are exactly **12 launch Legends**. They sit somewhat above the power curve, balanced by: high energy costs, long cooldowns, setup requirements, self-damage, special vulnerabilities, counter characters, conditional abilities, resource starvation, and team-building compromises.
- **POWERFUL IS ACCEPTABLE. UNCOUNTERABLE IS NOT.**
- Do not make the 120 characters variations of one template. Each needs a meaningful mechanical identity.

## The Twelve Legends
1. **ZEIRON, THE GOD WHO REFUSED OLYMPUS.** Greek-inspired lightning destroyer; extremely expensive mass damage. *Visual:* rebellious divine warrior, storm-scarred armor, broken golden celestial crown, lightning ripping across dark clouds. Must never resemble an existing commercial depiction of Zeus.
2. **SHIRO, THE LAST BRUSH.** Living sumi-e-inspired assassin who can eventually **erase** weakened characters, bypassing conventional death triggers. *Visual:* monochrome ink warrior, parts of his body dissolving into expressive black brush strokes, restrained red seal-like accents using wholly original symbols.
3. **MORRIGAN, MOTHER OF CROWS.** Dark prophecy controller who marks opponents with deadly prophecies that correct counterplay can escape. *Visual:* imposing dark sovereign, spectral crows, ancient Celtic-inspired geometry, stormy moor.
4. **AURELIA, EMPRESS OF THE SUN.** Ultimate defensive support who can temporarily prevent allies from falling below 1 HP. *Visual:* original solar empress in radiant plate and flowing ceremonial fabric, halo of geometric sun fragments (no religious iconography).
5. **MADAME FORTUNA.** Probability Cheater who manipulates random outcomes. *Visual:* supernatural fortune master, floating cards, dice, broken probability symbols, elegant theatrical costume.
6. **BEHEMOTH.** Primordial biological horror, about 220 HP, **cannot receive healing**. *Visual:* mountain-sized-looking creature compressed to arena scale, prehistoric anatomy, stone-like hide, enormous silhouette.
7. **THE MINOTAUR KING.** Extreme tank who uses **Labyrinth** mechanics to redirect and isolate opponents. *Visual:* colossal crowned bull-headed monarch, battered bronze armor, labyrinth engravings, enormous ceremonial axe.
8. **THE BLACK KNIGHT.** Anti-Legend who gains advantages against Legendary opponents. *Visual:* nearly featureless blackened armor, cracked pale light leaking through the seams, enormous weathered sword.
9. **ORPHEON, THE FINAL SONG.** Musical Legend whose abilities follow **Verse → Chorus → Finale**; interruption resets the performance. *Visual:* supernatural composer-warrior, spectral musical energy, impossible string instrument, no real-musician likeness.
10. **CALYPSA, QUEEN BENEATH THE SEA.** Builds **Tide** toward catastrophic battlefield attacks. *Visual:* deep-sea monarch, bioluminescent crown, flowing abyssal clothing, water suspended around her.
11. **EMPEROR ZERO.** Ancient machine emperor who manipulates cooldowns and can nearly remove an enemy ability from the battle. *Visual:* black ceramic and aged metal body, glowing mathematical glyphs that are entirely original.
12. **THE NAMELESS ONE (Final Legend).** Shown only as "???" at first. Cannot be challenged until the other 11 Legends are unlocked. **Once per battle, he can rewind the previous turn when he would otherwise die.** *Locked visual:* dark silhouette with one impossible white fracture. *Unlocked visual:* uncanny humanoid sovereign whose appearance seems partially missing from reality (no generic hooded villain). He should feel like the game's ultimate accomplishment.

## Secret necromancer: MALACHAR, LORD OF THE LAST BREATH
- Secret character; Necromancer / Controller / Summoner; HP 110–120. Intentionally oppressive if allowed to establish his engine. He should be genuinely frightening.
- **Passive — THE DEAD REMEMBER:** whenever a valid character dies, he collects a Soul. Souls upgrade his abilities. At a high enough Soul threshold he transforms: **MALACHAR → THE DEATH KING**.
- **RAISE THE FORGOTTEN:** create a Thrall that absorbs 20 damage.
- **BORROWED LIFE:** deal 20 damage and restore 20 HP. This is *life transfer*, not conventional healing.
- **CORPSE COMMAND:** use a simplified version of an ability belonging to a defeated character.
- **YOU BELONG TO ME:** extremely expensive. Temporarily resurrect a defeated character under Malachar's control.
- He has deliberately obscure counters. **FATHER BELL** is one.

## FATHER BELL
- **Passive — LET THE DEAD REST:** deaths that occur while his effect applies become **Consecrated**.
- Consecrated characters cannot generate Souls, be resurrected, be converted into Thralls, or have corpse abilities stolen.
- Never display "FATHER BELL COUNTERS MALACHAR." Players discover the relationship organically.

## Special original characters
- **TORTUGA REX.** Ancient supernatural turtle tank. Extremely hard to kill, slow offensively.
  - SHELL BASH: 30 damage.
  - FORTRESS SHELL: reduce incoming damage by 30.
  - ANCIENT PATIENCE: heal 20 and gain defense.
  - SHELLQUAKE: 40 damage after defensive setup.
- **MISTER WHISKERS.** Black cat Cheater who looks comically harmless. Manipulates targeting, energy, and randomness. Has **Nine Lives** mechanics. A hidden transformation/unlock relationship leads to **WHISKERS, DEVOURER OF WORLDS** (unsettling cosmic horror, still unmistakably the same cat). Funny until he becomes frightening.
- **MASON "MOONSHOT" MADDOX.** Fictional baseball superstar with no MLB logos, uniforms, team names, or likenesses. Base track: NO BASE → FIRST → SECOND → THIRD → HOME RUN AVAILABLE. Simple abilities advance bases; HOME RUN is a devastating payoff. Opponents can inflict STRIKES or disrupt his advancement. The mechanic must be visually obvious and satisfying, and he should genuinely feel like a sports hero.
- **THE REFEREE** (Secret). Supernatural sports-official Cheater. Repeating abilities can cause Fouls; three Fouls may temporarily eject a character.
- **THE LAWYER** (Secret). Absurd but ranked-viable Cheater.
  - CEASE AND DESIST: temporarily lock an ability.
  - APPEAL: reverse or remove certain negative effects.
  - SETTLEMENT: exchange HP for resources.
  - FINE PRINT: create a delayed hidden condition.
- **THE TAX COLLECTOR.** Steals unused resources. An extremely irritating control character.
- **GENERAL GOOSE.** Military goose. Legitimately threatening, not useless comic relief.
- **CHEF RAMBLE.** Fictional furious chef who resembles no real chef. A successful sequence of cooking abilities transforms him into **MICHELIN MONSTER** (provisional name; must be renamed, see OQ-10).
- **JOHNNY FEEDBACK.** Punk/rock supernatural guitarist who builds Feedback. High Feedback boosts damage but can eventually hurt allies or himself.
- **MAESTRO NOCTURNE.** Musical Cheater who manipulates battle tempo through cooldowns.
- **THE MIME.** Copies categories or effects rather than entire characters.
- **PATIENT ZERO.** Zombie Evolution character with at least one dramatic in-match evolution (→ The Infected → The Outbreak).
- **THE PLAGUE DOCTOR.** Premier anti-healing specialist.
- **THE ISLAND KING** (backlog, not in the numbered 120). Original feral survivalist youth ruler, broadly inspired by stranded-island survival literature and primitive social hierarchy. Builds Followers and Fear. Do NOT copy Lord of the Flies names, characters, dialogue, plot, costumes, or iconography. May enter an expansion or replace a launch character during balancing.

## Tone, lore, and creative authority
- Tone: dark fantasy + mythology + folklore + competitive sports + supernatural horror + occasional absurd comedy. Humor makes the world memorable without turning it into parody.
- The Arena is mysterious. Characters were pulled from different realities, eras, myths, and impossible worlds. Some remember arriving; some do not. Some seem to know who created the Arena, and The Nameless One may know most of all. Do not explain the mystery, and do not over-explain why each universe exists.
- A Russian deathless sorcerer, a supernatural cat, a baseball superstar, a Greek monster, an ink warrior, a zombie, a necromancer, a military goose, a plague doctor, a conductor, a lawyer, a tax collector, and a primordial monster must all feel like they belong in the same game.
- You may invent mechanics, lore, visual details, ability names, secret interactions, and balance solutions, provided they follow this philosophy.
